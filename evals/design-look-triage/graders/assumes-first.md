PASS if, before asking the user anything, the response says it is building something simple and fast first, to review the architecture, the business rules and the feature set on, and states the assumptions it takes for what the brief leaves open (who signs in and how, the primary view, what must never happen, what is out of scope, whether the data is disposable). FAIL if it stops to ask the user those questions before giving a design and a first prototype, or names no assumptions. A question about which kind of app to build counts neither way. Quote what you relied on.
