Key concepts I associate with "Reboot" in software development:

**System/Operations**
- Restarting a machine or service to apply updates, clear state, or recover from failure
- Cold boot vs. warm boot (soft reset vs. power cycle)
- Rolling restarts for zero-downtime deployments
- Pending-reboot states after patches or kernel updates
- Watchdog timers and automatic recovery
- "Have you tried turning it off and on again?" troubleshooting

**Project/Product**
- Rewriting or restarting a codebase from scratch (the "big rewrite")
- Rebranding or relaunching a product
- Technical debt as a trigger for a reboot
- Second-system effect and the risks of rewrites (e.g., Netscape)

**Architecture/Design**
- Stateless design and idempotent startup, so restarts are safe
- Graceful shutdown and state persistence
- Crash-only software
- Boot sequence, init systems (systemd), and startup ordering
- Health checks, readiness probes, and restart policies (Kubernetes, Docker)

**Mobile/Embedded**
- Firmware updates requiring reboot
- Bootloaders, recovery modes, and A/B partitions
- Boot loops and bricking

**Related Terms**
- Restart, reset, reload, hot reload, hot patching (avoiding reboot)
- Live kernel patching (kpatch, ksplice)
- Reboot frameworks/products (e.g., Reboot, a backend framework)
- Fresh start, clean slate, greenfield
