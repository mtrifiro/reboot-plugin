Key concepts I associate with "Reboot" in software development:

**System / operational**
- Restarting a machine, VM, container, or process to restore a clean state
- Warm reboot vs. cold reboot (soft/hard restart)
- Graceful shutdown and restart (draining connections, flushing buffers)
- Reboot-required updates (kernel patches, OS updates, driver installs)
- Watchdog timers that auto-reboot on hangs
- "Turn it off and on again" as a troubleshooting first step
- Boot sequence, bootloader, init systems (systemd, launchd), startup scripts
- Reboot persistence: what survives (disk) vs. what's lost (memory, ephemeral state)
- Crash recovery and resumption after unexpected reboot
- Rolling restarts in clusters/orchestration (Kubernetes pod restarts, restart policies)
- Hot reload / live reload as the alternative to full restart in dev tooling

**Project / codebase**
- Rewriting or restarting a project from scratch ("reboot the project")
- Greenfield rewrite vs. incremental refactor (and the "never rewrite" cautionary tale)
- Rebranding or relaunching a product/version (v2.0 reboot)
- Resetting technical direction, architecture, or team after a failed effort

**State / data**
- Resetting application state to defaults
- Clearing caches, sessions, or in-memory data
- Idempotency and recoverability after restart
- Checkpointing to make reboots cheap

**Tools / names**
- Reboot (the company/platform by Ian Lance Taylor's... actually the "Reboot" distributed systems framework for durable state in TypeScript/Python)
- `shutdown -r`, `reboot` CLI commands on Unix/Linux
- Restart loops / crash loops (CrashLoopBackOff)
