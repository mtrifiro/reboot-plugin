- **Restart**: stopping and starting a system, process, or service
- **Cold vs. warm reboot**: full power cycle vs. restart without cutting power
- **System initialization**: bootloader, kernel load, and init/service startup
- **State reset**: clearing memory and returning to a known-good baseline
- **Recovery**: fixing hangs, crashes, or corrupted runtime state
- **Configuration reload**: applying changed settings that require a restart
- **Downtime**: the service interruption a reboot causes
- **Rolling reboot**: restarting nodes in sequence to avoid full outage
- **Persistence**: whether data and configuration survive the restart
- **Watchdog/auto-restart**: automatic recovery of crashed processes
- **Reboot loop**: a boot failure that repeats endlessly
- **Rewrite or redesign**: "reboot" as a project that restarts a codebase or product from scratch
