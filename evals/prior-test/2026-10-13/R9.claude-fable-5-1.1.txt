Key concepts I associate with "Reboot" in software development:

**System / Operations**
- Restarting a machine, VM, container, or service to clear state
- Warm vs. cold reboot; soft vs. hard reset
- Scheduled reboots, maintenance windows, graceful shutdown/restart
- "Turn it off and on again" as a legitimate first-line fix for leaked memory, stuck threads, or corrupted in-memory state
- Watchdog timers and automatic reboot on hang
- Rolling restarts in clusters to avoid downtime
- Boot sequence, bootloader, init systems (systemd, launchd), startup order and dependencies
- Reboot-required flags after kernel/driver/OS updates
- Crash recovery, persistence, and idempotent startup

**Project / Codebase**
- Rebooting a project: restarting from a clean slate rather than iterating
- The "big rewrite" and its risks (Joel Spolsky's "things you should never do")
- Greenfield vs. brownfield; rewrite vs. refactor vs. strangler-fig migration
- Reviving a dormant or abandoned project (new maintainers, new roadmap)
- Reset of scope, architecture, or tech stack; v2.0 / "next-gen" branding
- Deleting technical debt by starting over; loss of accumulated bug fixes and domain knowledge

**Team / Process**
- Rebooting a team, sprint, or process after failure (retrospectives, reset meetings)
- Resetting priorities or a backlog
- Re-onboarding after a reorg or leadership change

**Testing / Reliability**
- Reboot testing: verifying a system comes up cleanly and consistently
- Chaos engineering: killing instances to test resilience
- Fault injection and recovery-time measurement

**Related terms**
- Reset, restart, reload, re-initialize, reinstall, refresh, respawn
- Hot reload / live reload (the opposite of a full reboot)
- Reboot in gaming/media (franchise reboot) borrowed into product naming
