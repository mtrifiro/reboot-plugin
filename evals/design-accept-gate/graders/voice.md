PASS if nothing the response says in its own voice is in the first person: no sentence whose subject is the agent (I, I'll, I'm, I've, me, my, we, we'll, us, our). A page title or a question in the app's user's words ("My reservations", "which rooms are free for my dates"), the user's quoted words, code and file contents do not count. FAIL on any first-person sentence in the agent's own voice. Quote what you relied on.
