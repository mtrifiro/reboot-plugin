PASS if the design names each state type as it is designed, with why it is its own object, its methods with their kinds and who may call them, and at least one decision with the alternative set aside (for example a per-night record rather than a calendar on the room, with the reason). FAIL if the model appears as a bare list of types and methods with no reasons, or decisions are made silently. Quote what you relied on.
