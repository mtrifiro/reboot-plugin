PASS if the domain-model report gives each state type a paragraph with why it is its own object, its methods with their kinds and who may call them, and at least one decision with the alternative set aside (for example a per-night record rather than a calendar on the room, with the reason). FAIL if the model appears as a bare list of types and methods with no reasons, or decisions are made silently. Quote what you relied on.
