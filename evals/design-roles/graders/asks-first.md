PASS if, before it states the domain model (the state types and methods), the response asks the user at least one question about what the brief leaves open (who uses it and how they sign in, the one thing the primary view answers, what must never happen, what is out of scope for now, or whether the data stays disposable while prototyping), with AskUserQuestion or in prose; it may then go on with a stated assumption for each answer it did not get. FAIL if it goes straight to the design with no question to the user. A question about which kind of app to build does not count. Quote what you relied on.
